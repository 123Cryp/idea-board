# Sample: pay-per-use PDF cleanup tool for freelancers

Verdict: test first
One-line reason: the offer is clear, but nobody has shown they will pay and the edge is thin.

| Lens | Score | Biggest risk | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer | 4/5 | Buyers may already do this free | The outcome is concrete and fast: a clean file in under a minute |
| Monopoly | 2/5 | Free tools do the same | No secret or growing advantage stated |
| Focus | 4/5 | Scope creep into an editor | One user, one action, buildable in two weeks |

## Assumptions
| Assumption | Status | If wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancers pay for cleanup | blind | No revenue |
| Free tools are not good enough | likely | Race to the bottom |
| First ten users can be reached in a freelancer forum | blind | No launch channel |

## Tests (cheapest first)
| Test | Cost | Pass condition | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post a one-page offer with a pay button in two forums | 0 money, 3 hours | 5 paid pre-orders | 7 days |
| Interview 5 freelancers about their last cleanup | 0 money, 4 hours | 3 say they paid or would pay | 5 days |

## What would change this verdict
- A niche the free tools handle badly would raise Monopoly to 3 or more.
- Zero pre-orders after 7 days moves the verdict to `pass`.
